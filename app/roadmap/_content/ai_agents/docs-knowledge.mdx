Keeps the "big picture" of your software clear by automatically mapping dependencies and component roles.

- **Dependency Mapping:** Visualizes how different modules interact using Mermaid diagrams.
- **Component Auditing:** Generates a high-level inventory of responsibilities for each directory.
- **Onboarding Speed:** Dramatically reduces the time needed for new developers to understand the project structure.
