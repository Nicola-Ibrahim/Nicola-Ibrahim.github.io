---
title: "4. Pub/Sub & The Event Bus (1-to-Many)"
shortDesc: "The Publish/Subscribe paradigm for broadcasting facts to multiple independent listeners."
---

Pub/Sub (Publish/Subscribe) is an asynchronous communication paradigm where senders do not target specific receivers.

- **🧠 The Mindset:** \"I am announcing a fact. I don't care who is listening.\"
- **🎯 Use Case:** Real-time dashboards, user notifications, or cross-service updates.
- **🚀 Decoupling:** Producers never need to know about Consumers.
