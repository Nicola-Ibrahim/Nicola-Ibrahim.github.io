Dynamic Programming solves complex problems by caching the results of overlapping subproblems.

- **Memoization (Top-Down):** Start with a recursive tree and cache the results to prevent duplicate work.
- **Tabulation (Bottom-Up):** Build the solution iteratively using an array or matrix.
- **Identifying DP:** Look for questions asking for the "maximum/minimum" or "number of ways" involving overlapping decisions.
</Topic>

>
Greedy algorithms pick the best immediate option without looking ahead, hoping it leads to a global optimum.

- **Local Optimums:** Making the most profitable choice at the current step.
- **Interval Scheduling:** Heavily used for sorting intervals by start or end times to find overlaps.
- **The Challenge:** Proving mathematically that the greedy choice will not trap you in a sub-optimal solution.
</Topic>
