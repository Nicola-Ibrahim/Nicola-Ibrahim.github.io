Linked lists test your ability to manage references and memory pointers without losing data.

- **Dummy Nodes:** An essential trick to cleanly handle edge cases (like modifying the head of the list).
- **Tortoise and Hare:** Using a fast (2x) and slow (1x) pointer to detect cycles or find the middle element.
- **In-Place Reversal:** Carefully swapping `.next` pointers without allocating new memory.
</Topic>

Stacks (Last-In-First-Out) are crucial for parsing, while Queues (First-In-First-Out) manage sequences.

- **Parsing & State:** Stacks are perfect for validating parentheses or evaluating Reverse Polish Notation.
- **Monotonic Stacks:** Maintain strictly increasing or decreasing elements to find the "next greater element" in O(N) time.
- **Queues:** Used for BFS and managing sequential processing.
</Topic>
