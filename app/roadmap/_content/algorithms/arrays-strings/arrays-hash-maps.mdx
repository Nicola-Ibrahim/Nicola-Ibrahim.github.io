Hash maps trade space for time, giving you O(1) lookups. This is usually the first optimization step in brute-force O(N^2) array problems.

- **Frequency Counters:** Count occurrences of elements to quickly solve anagram or duplicate problems.
- **Prefix Sums:** Pre-calculate running totals to optimize subarray computations.
- **Index Mapping:** Store elements alongside their indices for fast retrieval (e.g., Two Sum).
