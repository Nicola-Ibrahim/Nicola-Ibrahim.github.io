Tries store characters in nodes, making them incredibly fast for word validation and prefix searches.

- **O(L) Time Complexity:** Searches take time proportional to the length of the word (L), not the number of words.
- **Autocomplete Systems:** The foundational data structure behind search bar suggestions.
- **Efficient Storage:** Shares common prefixes to save memory compared to Hash Sets.
