Heaps are complete binary trees that satisfy the heap property, used to quickly fetch the largest or smallest element.

- **O(1) Min/Max Access:** Instantly retrieve the highest priority element.
- **Top K Problems:** The most efficient way to find the Kth largest element in a stream.
- **Running Medians:** Using a Max-Heap and Min-Heap together to track medians dynamically.
