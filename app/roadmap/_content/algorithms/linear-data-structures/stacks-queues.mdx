Stacks (Last-In-First-Out) are crucial for parsing, while Queues (First-In-First-Out) manage sequences.

- **Parsing & State:** Stacks are perfect for validating parentheses or evaluating Reverse Polish Notation.
- **Monotonic Stacks:** Maintain strictly increasing or decreasing elements to find the \"next greater element\" in O(N) time.
- **Queues:** Used for BFS and managing sequential processing.
