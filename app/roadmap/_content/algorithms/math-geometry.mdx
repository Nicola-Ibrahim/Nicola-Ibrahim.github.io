>
Bitwise operations are executed directly by the CPU ALU, making them incredibly fast and memory efficient.

- **XOR Magic:** `x ^ x = 0`. Incredibly powerful for finding unique, non-repeating elements.
- **Shifting:** `<<` and `>>` physically move bits to multiply or divide by 2 instantly.
- **Masking:** Using AND (`&`) operators to count the number of 1s or isolate specific bits.
</Topic>
