Greedy algorithms pick the best immediate option without looking ahead, hoping it leads to a global optimum.

- **Local Optimums:** Making the most profitable choice at the current step.
- **Interval Scheduling:** Heavily used for sorting intervals by start or end times to find overlaps.
- **The Challenge:** Proving mathematically that the greedy choice will not trap you in a sub-optimal solution.
