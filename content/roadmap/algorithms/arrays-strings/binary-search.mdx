Binary search isn't just for finding elements in a sorted array; it's a powerful divide-and-conquer strategy.

- **O(log N) Time:** Slashes search space in half at every step.
- **Search on Answer:** Used to find optimal solutions in a range (e.g., Koko Eating Bananas).
- **Key Challenge:** Identifying the monotonic property and managing left/right pointer edge cases.
