Two Pointers are used when traversing sorted arrays or finding pairs. Sliding Window is a subset used to find contiguous subarrays.

- **Opposite Ends:** Move left and right pointers inward to find pairs (e.g., Valid Palindrome).
- **Sliding Window:** Expand the window by moving the right pointer, and shrink it by moving the left pointer.
- **Optimization:** Converts nested O(N^2) loops into linear O(N) time.
